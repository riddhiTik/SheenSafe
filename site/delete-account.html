Delete your HeatGuard Kashmir account

This page explains how to delete your account and data for the HeatGuard Kashmir app (developer: SheenSafe).

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open HeatGuard Kashmir.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately and the app returns to the setup screen.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email riddhi.tikoo@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my HeatGuard account". We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email address and password)
  • Your profile: name, age and language
  • Emergency contact names and phone numbers
  • Your risk profile, heat-risk assessments and wearable sensor readings (heart rate, temperature)
  • All data stored locally on your device

What is kept

Nothing linked to your account is kept after deletion. Routine server backups may hold copies for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.

HeatGuard Kashmir is decision-support software, not a medical device.
