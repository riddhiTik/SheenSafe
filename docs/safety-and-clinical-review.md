# Safety and clinical review

## Product claim

Use: early heat-risk warning and escalation aid.

Do not use: diagnosis, heatstroke detector, medical monitor, medical replacement, or guarantee of prevention.

## Emergency language

Emergency state should instruct:

- Move to shade or a cooler place.
- Stop activity.
- Loosen or remove extra clothing.
- Cool with water, wet cloths, fan, or ice/cold packs where available.
- Contact emergency help or a trained worker.
- Do not leave the person alone.
- Give small sips of water only if awake and able to swallow.

## Pilot rules

- Do not intentionally expose people to dangerous heat for testing.
- Use conservative thresholds at first.
- Treat confusion, fainting, seizure, inability to stand, and nonresponse as emergency-level events.
- Review all false emergency alerts and all missed high-risk symptom reports.
- Keep a human escalation process active during the pilot.

## Required review

- Local clinician or public-health reviewer.
- Humanitarian protection/privacy reviewer.
- Local language reviewer.
- Volunteer operations lead.
